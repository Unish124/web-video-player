      Add video from URL

      Paste a YouTube link (youtube.com or youtu.be) and it's auto-detected and embedded with YouTube's own player. Other links must be direct video files (.mp4, .webm, .ogg) that the host allows browsers to fetch — it won't work with Netflix or other streaming site pages.

      Extra features

      Off by default to keep things simple — switch on whichever you want.

      Resume playback
      Remembers where you left off on saved video links and picks back up there next time.
      Multiple playlists
      Create extra named playlists in the Saved tab, beyond the default one.
      A/B loop
      Adds a button to mark two points and repeat that section on loop.
      Study timer
      Adds a countdown timer button that pauses the video when time's up. Drag its header to move it, drag the corner to resize it.
      Customize player controls
      Show or hide individual buttons and elements in the player, one by one.
